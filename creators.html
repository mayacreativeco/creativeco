The Crash Course

Six modules, 45 minutes or more each, built as follow-along pages: the video on the left, a checklist that ticks off as you hit each step on the right, project files underneath. You edit while you watch. By the end you can build any of the six from scratch — and the seventh one you invent yourself.

1
The Layer Cake — CapCut decoded. Your first full edit start to export.
2
Read the Footage — the story before the cut.
3
Un-Scrollable — captions first, cut on the feeling, retention pass.
4
Not the Default — type that serves. A caption system you reuse forever.
5
Layers on Layers — overlays, masks, keyframes. The sunglasses-reflection edit rebuilt live.
6
Something That Shouldn't Exist — AI that doesn't look like AI.

The Rough Cut

A monthly editing retainer. You shoot, I cut. I hold the creative direction and curate a visual identity that's unmistakably yours — captions, color, pacing, type — so every post looks like it came from the same person, because it did.

The edits
Five to twenty videos a month, finished and ready to post, delivered within five business days.
The direction
A monthly call on what you're making and why. I keep the thread between posts so you don't have to.
The identity
A living style guide — your caption system, presets and type — built from your first month and refined every month after.

Who it's for: you're posting consistently, the footage is good, and editing is the thing eating your week.
