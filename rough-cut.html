what you send

Your footage, dropped into a shared folder each week. The posts you keep saving. A voice note about what you were going for. That's it — no brief templates.

what I do

Edit five to twenty videos a month, captions first, cut on the feeling. Hold the creative direction between posts. Build the caption, color and type system you post in — and keep it consistent when you'd have drifted.

what you get

Finished, ready-to-post edits within five business days. A living style guide for your feed. A monthly direction call. One person who knows your whole body of work, not just this week's clip.

for you if

You're posting consistently, the footage is good, and editing is the thing eating your week. You want a look that's recognisably yours without thinking about it every Tuesday night.

not for you if

How a month runs.

  1. 1The direction callMonth one: 45 minutes on who you are on camera and what your feed should feel like. After that, 20 minutes a month to keep the thread.
  2. 2The dropYou upload footage weekly. I tell you within a day what's usable and what to reshoot before I've cut a frame.
  3. 3The cutEdits back within five business days, with one round of notes. Captions, color and type pulled from your style guide, not from whatever's trending.
  4. 4The reviewEnd of month: what performed, what we change, what we shoot next. The style guide gets a version number.
