Terms

3-month minimum. 2 revision rounds per video. 5-business-day turnaround. Retainers billed monthly in advance.

what you send

Your footage, dropped into a shared folder each week. The posts you keep saving. A voice note about what you were going for. That's it — no brief templates.

what I do

Edit five to twenty-plus videos a month, captions first, cut on the feeling. Hold the creative direction between posts. Build the caption, color and type system you post in — and keep it consistent when you'd have drifted.

what you get

Finished, ready-to-post edits within five business days. A living style guide for your feed. A monthly direction call. One person who knows your whole body of work, not just this week's clip.
